05 / BEFORE WE BEGIN

Questions worth asking.

Can my team still respond personally?

Yes. We define when a person steps in and how they receive context. The handoff is tested as part of the agreed process.

Do I need to replace all my tools?

We first review what you already use. We confirm compatibility, permissions, and scope before proposing an integration.

Who owns my customer information?

Your business retains ownership. Access, use, and delivery of data are defined in the service agreement.

Does a consultation commit me to purchasing?

No. We review your situation and confirm whether we can help. You receive the scope and investment to decide before implementation.
